Is this just translated English content?

No. Each piece is written natively for its language and audience, targeting the keywords Spanish speakers actually search — which are often different from the English ones. Translation ranks poorly and reads worse; we don't do it.

Who is this for?

Local businesses with Hispanic customers or ambitions: contractors, dental clinics, law firms, realtors, restaurants, home services — especially in TX, FL, CA, NY, IL, and NJ.

What if I don't like the first article?

We rewrite it free. If the rewrite still doesn't convince you, we refund it. Simple.
