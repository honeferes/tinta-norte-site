Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Who we are

Tinta Norte Content Studio ("Tinta Norte", "we") is a content studio based in Stamford, Connecticut. This policy explains what information we collect through tintanortestudio.com and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • When you request a free brief: your name, business name, city and state, website (if provided), and an email address or WhatsApp number.
  • When you email or message us: the content of your message and your contact details.
  • Payments: processed by Stripe. We never see or store your full card details.

How we use it

We use your information only to respond to your request, prepare your free brief, provide our services, and send invoices. We do not use it for advertising lists, and we never sell or rent it to anyone.

Who processes it for us

Our contact form is delivered through Web3Forms and our email runs on Zoho Mail; this site is hosted on GitHub Pages. These providers process your data only so we can receive your messages.

Cookies

This website does not set tracking or advertising cookies.

Retention and your rights

We keep inquiry and client records only as long as needed for the business relationship and legal obligations. You can ask us anytime what we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by writing to hola@tintanortestudio.com.
